Aerial Vehicles

Since the summer of 2011, I have been building aerial vehicles as a means to satiate my passion for aviation. Over the years, my projects have included fixed-wing RC planes of varying wing configurations and quadcopters.


While most of them were powered by brushless motors, some were powered by nitromethane IC engines.


I will keep this page straightforward and put out snapshots of the aerial vehicles I built. Please note, these are only some of the drones and planes I built—not all were documented.
