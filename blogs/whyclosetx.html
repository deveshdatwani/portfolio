Why ClosetX?

Published: May 15, 2025

Fashion is fascinating personally. I love how creative one can get styling up.



What is ClosetX?


Think that you're a student or maybe just fancy rationality, thoughtful spending, and frugality.


You buy clothes during sales, or look for used apparel across different services.


You scroll through online stores and look at models styled up immaculately. You admire it, and once in a while, you "buy the look"—but it almost always costs more than what you're happy to pay.


Sometimes, you buy clothes and don't wear them as often as you'd like, or return them, because they didn't suit your style and there was no real way of assessing this beforehand.



This is where ClosetX can help. Say:


  • You have 3 trousers in your wardrobe
  • You have a couple of t-shirts
  • You're looking to shop for new outfits

How do you pick a t-shirt or a shirt that best suits your current wardrobe? Can the t-shirt you buy be mixed and matched with other apparel in your wardrobe to create multiple outfits? Can you buy one pair of trousers and one t-shirt that creates 4 different looks when combined with your existing wardrobe portfolio?

ClosetX helps you do just that!


At its core, ClosetX is powered by a vision transformer that helps you style up cost-effectively. It periodically learns fashion sense from humans and adapts to your preferences. It's essentially your personalized AI fashion stylist—the friend you send pictures of apparel to when asking if you should buy them.


P.S. Do send that picture to your friend anyway!

That's how you can shop frugally, rationally, and make the absolute most out of every dollar you spend.
