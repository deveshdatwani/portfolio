What is P(doom) ?


In the AI safety field, P(doom) is the probability of existentially catastrophic outcomes (so-called "doomsday scenarios") as a result of artificial intelligence. [1]


Over the past year, P(doom) has been discussed quite avidly in my work and personal environment. And the consensus is leaning towards a very high value of P(doom) in the next decade. Outcomes such as partial / total economic collapse, hostile takeover or similar.


The argument is that there's no skill at which humans can outperform a frontier model, or at least future frontier models. So what makes us think there can't be a hostile take over once super intelligence is able to hack its supply chain? From mining rare earth to producing GPU chips.


Said argument concludes that the silicon stack is potentially far superior to the bio stack.


Although we can self-sustain ourselves using natural energy sources and the organic food pyramid which begins with sunlight hitting a plant, we are vulnerable at far too many stages. For instance, it takes 9 months to reproduce a human, which by no means is even close to being able to defend itself let alone join the resistance against machines after birth. Humans are also vulnerable to biohazards which can be weaponized by super intelligence.


This is now beginning to sound like a James Cameron plot. Personally, it sounds more thrilling than scary.
